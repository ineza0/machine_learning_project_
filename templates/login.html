ML Predictor

A student prediction and academic analysis platform designed to help students understand their learning performance and estimate their academic outcome.

What does ML Predictor do?

ML Predictor uses Machine Learning to analyze information such as study hours, attendance, assignments, CAT and practical performance. Based on the information provided, the system predicts whether a student is likely to PASS or FAIL.
