STEP BY STEP

How to Use ML Predictor

Follow these 6 simple steps and get your result in minutes.

1

Create your account

Click "Create account" in the login box below. Enter a username, email and a strong password, or simply use Google to sign up in seconds.

2

Sign in

Enter your username and password and click "Sign in →", or click "Continue with Google". You will be taken to your personal dashboard.

3

Choose a subject

In the dashboard, enter or select the subject you want to analyze (for example Mathematics, Physics or Networking).

4

Fill in your information

Enter your study hours, attendance, assignments, CAT and practical marks. The more accurate your data, the better the prediction.

5

Click "Predict"

The Machine Learning model analyzes your data and gives you a PASS or FAIL result with its probability.

6

Improve and try again

If the result is not good, study more or attend more classes, then try again. Your full prediction history is saved to your account.

Yes. Passwords are stored securely and are never displayed on the website.

No. It is an estimate based on the information you enter. It helps you prepare but does not decide your exam results.
